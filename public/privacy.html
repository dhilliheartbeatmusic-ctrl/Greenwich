ADMISSION ENQUIRIES

Your enquiry.
Your information.

This notice describes the information requested by the Greenwich Global Institute of Hotel Management website.

What you choose to share

The form asks for your name, mobile number, request type, study area, qualification, preferred career direction, optional email and message. Course booking requests also ask for a preferred start month. Consent and receipt timestamps are stored with a request reference. Please do not include identity documents, payment information, medical information or other sensitive details in the message field.

How an enquiry is delivered

If the form says “Send admission enquiry”, “Send booking request” or “Send career enquiry”, your details are submitted to the institute’s website server and made available to authorised admissions staff. You receive a reference when the server confirms receipt.

If the form says “Continue on WhatsApp”, it prepares a message. Nothing is delivered until you open WhatsApp and send it. WhatsApp processes that message under its own privacy terms.

Why the information is requested

The admissions team uses your enquiry to answer your questions and contact you about courses, fees, visits, admission requirements, booking requests and shore or ship career directions. A request does not confirm admission, reserve a seat, or guarantee employment. No payment is collected by the website.

Access and retention

Website enquiries are accessible through a password-protected staff dashboard. Staff can update enquiry status, export records and remove enquiries that are no longer needed. Contact the institute to request correction or deletion of your enquiry, or to ask about retention.

Cookies and third-party services

The website code includes no advertising or analytics trackers. The staff dashboard uses an essential sign-in cookie. External services, including WhatsApp and the website hosting provider, may process data under their own policies.
