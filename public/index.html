BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Your questions.
Clear answers.

Speak to admissions ↗
How do I book a course?+

Choose a study area and select “Book this course”. Complete the request with your qualification, preferred start month and contact details. The team will confirm course availability, fees and admission steps. A request is not a confirmed seat or payment.

Which courses and qualifications are available?+

Contact admissions for the current official course list, qualification awarded, awarding body, eligibility and duration. Course cards introduce the areas you can discuss with our team.

Can I explore both shore and ship careers?+

Yes. Choose “Career enquiry” and select Shore, Ship or Either. Tell us your qualification and the role that interests you. Ship employment requirements depend on the employer, vessel and role.

Are these live vacancies or guaranteed jobs?+

No. The careers section describes possible directions. Ask about current opportunities and course-specific career support. Employment, salaries, internships and overseas placements are not guaranteed.

Can parents visit the institute?+

Call +91 99676 25438 or request a campus conversation. The team will confirm the full campus address, a suitable time and availability.

Can I pay course fees on this website?+

No online payments are collected. Confirm official fees, payment instructions and receipt arrangements directly with the institute before paying.
