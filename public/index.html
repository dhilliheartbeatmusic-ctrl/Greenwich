GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

A little clarity
before you begin.

Speak to our team ↗
Which courses can I apply for?+

Please contact admissions for the current course list, qualifications awarded, availability and intake dates. The study areas on this website help you explore your interests.

What are the eligibility requirements?+

Eligibility depends on the course. Tell admissions your highest completed qualification and the course you are interested in to get the applicable requirements.

How much are the course fees?+

Request the current written fee structure from admissions. Ask about tuition, examination charges, uniforms, equipment, accommodation and any instalment options that may apply.

Are internships and placements available?+

Ask admissions about the specific support and arrangements for your selected course. This website does not promise a job, internship, salary or overseas placement.

Can parents visit the institute?+

Call or send a WhatsApp enquiry to request a visit. Our team can confirm the full address, suitable timing and availability.

Does an enquiry confirm my admission?+

No. An enquiry is a request for information. Admission is subject to the institute’s official process, eligibility checks and confirmation.
